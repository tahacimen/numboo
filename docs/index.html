Support

Numboo is a fast-paced number-reflex game. Find the target number, keep your combo alive, and avoid a wrong tap.

Need help?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Information stored on your device

Numboo stores game progress only on your device, including your best score, badges, power-up inventory, daily-goal progress, achievements, and sound and language preferences. This information is not sent to our servers and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Accounts and personal information

Numboo does not require an account, login, name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photos to play.

Advertising and purchases

The first released version of Numboo contains no advertising, analytics SDK, or in-app purchases. If a future version adds optional rewarded advertising or a Premium purchase, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before that version is released. Advertising will never be required to play the core game.

Children

Numboo is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
